Executive Problem Statement

Why Traditional Bank Telemarketing Was Burning Capital

Outbound phone campaigns are labor-intensive. When campaigns run blind without predictive lead scoring, agents spend 89% of their time hearing "No", bleeding operating budget and fatiguing the customer base.

88.7% Wasted Call Volume

Out of 41,188 outbound dials, only 4,640 (11.27%) resulted in a term deposit subscription. Random dialing resulted in 36,548 wasted conversations on uninterested customers.

Class Ratio: 7.88 Negatives per 1 Positive

Severe Seasonal Misallocation

The bank concentrated over 45% of its total call volume during May and July—months with dreadful conversion rates (<9%). Meanwhile, top-converting autumn/winter months (>43%) were starved of budget.

May: 6.4% conv. vs March: 50.5% conv.

Channel Blindness & Fatigue

Substantial dial time was spent calling landlines (5.23% conversion), whereas cellular outreach converts at 14.74% (a 2.82x multiplier). Calling beyond 3 attempts resulted in severe fatigue (<2.1% conversion).

Cellular 2.82x higher than Landline

The Critical Data Leakage Trap: Call "Duration"

In historical call logs, the feature duration (call length in seconds) exhibits extreme correlation with sale success (r = 0.405). If a customer agrees to buy, the call naturally lasts 15 minutes to explain contracts; if they reject, they hang up in 10 seconds.

The Trap: Call duration is completely unknown before dialing. Including it produces an artificial test ROC-AUC of 0.9472, but creates an operational model that is 100% useless in production because telemarketers need to rank prospects prior to picking up the telephone!

Technical Mitigations

What We Have Solved: 5 Complex Data Science Hurdles

Overcoming subtle data traps in retail banking CRM systems required rigorous hypothesis testing, cost-sensitive algorithms, and domain-informed feature transformations.

CHALLENGE 01

Duration Target Leakage

The Obstacle: Call duration produces an illusory 0.947 ROC-AUC that cannot score leads before dialing.
Our Solution: Discarded duration completely from the operational pipeline. Engineered prior behavioral memory features (recency, prior outcome) to recover legitimate predictive power.
CHALLENGE 02

Severe Class Imbalance (88.7% vs 11.3%)

The Obstacle: Standard loss functions learn to predict 'No' continuously to achieve 88.7% useless accuracy.
Our Solution: Deployed cost-sensitive weights (scale_pos_weight = 7.87 in LightGBM/XGBoost) and calibrated decision thresholds from 0.50 down to 0.28 to maximize F1-score and minority recall.
CHALLENGE 03

Macro Multicollinearity (ρ = 0.972)

The Obstacle: euribor3m, emp.var.rate, and nr.employed are near-perfectly collinear, inflating variance in standard regressions.
Our Solution: Applied L2 ridge shrinkage to linear models and deployed histogram-based Gradient Boosted Trees (LightGBM) which split greedily without matrix inversion instability.
CHALLENGE 04

Masked Missing Values ('unknown')

The Obstacle: Standard .isnull() audits returned 0, yet over 20% of credit defaults and housing statuses were encoded as 'unknown'.
Our Solution: Preserved 'unknown' as an explicit informative category. Proved statistically that clients with unknown credit default convert at 2.5x lower rates than clients with verified good credit.
CHALLENGE 05

Bimodal pdays Artifact (96.3% = 999)

The Obstacle: 96.3% of the pdays column contains the code 999 (client never contacted before), completely distorting continuous scaling.
Our Solution: Engineered a binary indicator pdays_contacted and partitioned recency into discrete cohorts (0-7 days, 8-14 days, 15+ days, never contacted).
CHALLENGE 06

Optimistic Cross-Validation Bias

The Obstacle: Data leakage can occur if scaling or one-hot encoders fit on validation splits.
Our Solution: Implemented strict Scikit-Learn Pipeline encapsulation with Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation, ensuring zero data contamination across all train/test boundaries.

Strategic Playbook

The 7-Pillar Strategic Action Playbook for Banking

How marketing leadership and call center supervisors operationalize this predictive model into daily workflows.

1

Strict Decile 1 & 2 Targeting

Pre-score all CRM contacts monthly. Route only leads scoring in the top two deciles to telemarketers. Discontinue cold outbound dials to the lower 80%.

+3.6x Higher Strike Rate
2

Seasonal Budget Pivot

Reallocate marketing headcount away from May and July (<9% conv.) toward March, September, October, and December (>43% conv.) to capture seasonal liquidity surges.

5.2x Seasonal Efficiency
3

Cellular-First Outreach Mandate

Mandate calls to verified mobile numbers (14.7% conv.) rather than fixed landlines (5.2% conv.). Prompt online banking and mobile app users to keep phone numbers updated.

2.82x Channel Multiplier
4

The "3-Call Fatigue" Rule

Enforce an automated CRM lock after 3 unsuccessful dials per campaign. Beyond 3 attempts, marginal conversion drops to 2% while generating negative brand fatigue.

Zero Agent Burnout
5

7-Day Warm Re-Engagement Queue

Customers who previously subscribed (poutcome='success') convert at 65.1%. Build an automated queue ensuring these warm leads are re-contacted within 3–7 days.

65.1% Repeat Conversion
6

Macro Counter-Cyclical Triggers

Trigger automated campaign pushes whenever the 3-month Euribor drops below 1.5% or market volatility increases—investors naturally seek fixed, guaranteed deposits.

Macro Market Timing
7

Persona-Specific Value Propositions

Pitch capital safety and pension supplement yields to seniors (>60 yrs, 45.5% conv.). Pitch automated mobile recurring savings to students (<25 yrs, 31.4% conv.).
